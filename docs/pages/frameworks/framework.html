Two foundations

An open skill set, and a structured way to ask questions.

The 5W1H method

A structured approach built on six fundamental questions: Who, What, When, Where, Why and How. Working through them systematically gives a community a comprehensive understanding of a situation or challenge, which leads to more effective solutions.

Applied to skills, the method maps what a community needs, who holds it, and how to close the gap.

The method in practice

Six questions, applied to a community's skills.

Integrating skillzeb.io with 5W1H lets a community map its unique skill requirements and address challenges in order — starting with why.

01 / Why

The rationale

Understand why these skills are needed. Clarify how developing them will benefit the community and contribute to its overarching objectives.

02 / What

The skills

Identify the specific skills needed within the community. Use skillzeb.io's Skill Set Explorer to pinpoint the ones that align with community goals.

03 / Who

The people

Determine who already possesses these skills and who requires them — recognising existing talent and identifying the individuals or groups who would benefit from development.

04 / When

The timeline

Establish timelines for skill development initiatives. Decide when programs should commence and set achievable milestones along the way.

05 / Where

The place

Ascertain where these skills are most needed — a specific project, a department, or a geographical area within the community.

06 / How

The strategy

Develop strategies for acquiring and applying the skills. Use skillzeb.io's Template Explorer to find or create templates that make learning and integration effective.
